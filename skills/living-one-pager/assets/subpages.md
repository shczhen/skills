# Subpage skeletons

Each subpage opens with a link back to the product page.

## Decisions

Append-only, newest first. To overturn a decision, add a row, then set the old row's standing to "superseded by" with the new row's date.

| Date | Decision | Reason or basis | Standing |
|-|-|-|-|
| YYYY-MM-DD, or "by YYYY-MM-DD" when the source gives no date | What was decided | Why, or where it was decided | in effect / superseded by YYYY-MM-DD |

Below the table, list questions that are still open, including decisions that the sources imply and nobody has stated.

## Plan

Target dates live only here. The page exists once a source gives a target date. When a milestone passes, fill in the outcome and keep the original target.

| Target date | Milestone | Outcome |
|-|-|-|
| YYYY-MM-DD | What should happen | What happened, or "not yet" |

## Sources

The source table comes first. It is what an update re-reads.

| Source | What it answers | Last read |
|-|-|-|
| The live product, repository, doc, or material, by its address: a URL, or a repository and path | Which facts on the page rest on it | YYYY-MM-DD, with what the source showed |

After the table: sources that could not be read, full evidence such as test methods and results, quoting the passage a fact rests on in place of the whole source, a table of points where materials disagree, discussion, and original conversations. Each section states what holds now, and a new read replaces the old text. Keep a user's messages verbatim under a dated heading; a summary sits beside the original and is labelled as a summary.
