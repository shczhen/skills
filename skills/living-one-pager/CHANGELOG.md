# Living One-pager changelog

## Skill 0.3.0 — 2026-11-05

Rewritten around a persistent product or capability, replacing development-round records as the organizing model.

- Lead with product definition, users and value, capabilities/use cases, and how to experience it. Keep progress, evidence, discussion and content materials connected to that explanation.
- Reuse the same product entry through development, releases, observations and iteration. Identify existing content affected by changes.
- Add independently versioned draft templates, document revisions and short per-page changelogs. Record future template changes and adaptation notes when those changes are defined; earlier drafts have no migration contract.
- Preserve existing storage choices, local/full defaults, original material, personal customizations, and solo use.

## Skill 0.2.0-draft.4 — 2026-11-05

Initial public trial. Gathered content inputs and retained source discussions, with a work-round record model. Replaced by the product model in 0.3.0.

## Template versions

### living-one-pager 0.1.0 — 2026-11-05

First explicitly versioned product draft template. Adds product explanation, users/value, capabilities/use cases, experience path, availability, evidence, materials, and a changelog. See [version guidance](references/versions.md); this draft does not define old-version migration paths.

### living-one-pager/communication-brief 0.1.0 — 2026-11-05

First explicitly versioned optional brief. Records its checked product-page revision and its own change history. Producing a brief remains optional.
