# Living One-pager changelog

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-16

Rebuilt from two real product pages. Earlier pages went stale and repeated the same fact in several sections.

- A fixed page skeleton in which every fact has one home. Status lives in one table, dates in the plan, decisions in an append-only record.
- A claims table, one row per subject: the sentence content can use, its basis and conditions, the wordings that go further than the basis, and a scope the maintainer confirms.
- Three jobs, each with steps: build a page, update it after a change, prepare content from it. An update follows the change through claims and materials and reports which materials are out of date.
- A keep-current line for the product repository's agent instructions, so product changes reach the page when they happen.
- Pages live in a team workspace with a catalog.

Removed: template versions, page revision numbers, and per-page changelogs; the communication brief; public-export guidance; the storage agreement dialogue. Pages written with 0.3.0 are moved into the new skeleton on their next update.
