# Working with product facts and materials

## Recover the product account

Read the relevant available conversation, page, document, or result. Extract a usable explanation: product definition, users, problem, value, capabilities, typical situations, experience path, availability, evidence, and material links. The entry may already contain most of this; improve it in place rather than creating another tracker.

For sessions, use the host's supported reader and relevant history. Retain user and assistant roles and identify which suggestions were adopted. If only an excerpt or summary is accessible, say what it covers; do not claim a complete transcript. Ask for an export only when needed, not a retelling of facts already available.

Read supplied files at their real locations. Preserve originals and their labels, including fictional or test data. Product event dates, ownership, availability, and results come from the sources; the page's creation/update date describes the document only. Unknown progress stays unknown, even when the directory is empty or the user calls something an idea.

## Collect what helps someone explain or show the product

| Need | Useful input |
|---|---|
| Understand it | A plain definition, intended users, situations, problems, and value. |
| Describe its behavior | Capabilities, representative scenarios, components and their relationships, limits, actual availability. |
| Try or demonstrate it | Existing entry point, usage steps, examples, demos, screenshots, recordings, documentation or code references. |
| Support a claim | Observations, comparisons, feedback or data, with source, sample and conditions when relevant. |
| Prepare content | The relevant material, actual draft/published/unknown status, what it supports, and what fact or artifact is missing. |

These are prompts for gathering material, not required fields. Prefer a useful first account and the next important input over a comprehensive interview. If the next input is a demo or usage steps, ask for the existing material; keep underlying development with the maker.

## Connect an overview and its content

A product page can contain internal discussion and link a concise public handout, blog, video, deck, or tutorial. It can also serve as the concise introduction itself. Follow existing organization; neither a second document nor a complete set of assets is mandatory.

Keep the explanation readable. Link detailed materials and show their purpose and actual status where useful. A supplied draft can be described as **draft; publication status unknown** unless a source confirms publication history. Likewise, missing other users' feedback means **feedback not provided**, not that no one has used or commented on the product. Fetch relevant linked content when needed to understand or update a claim; state unread or inaccessible coverage.

When a value statement or capability changes, identify the affected materials and the specific statement that needs attention. Different times, populations, prerequisites, or measurement endpoints can explain different numbers. Preserve each source's conditions and leave a concrete unresolved question rather than silently making the numbers equal.

## Storage and source coverage

Follow the accepted location, scope, and detail level. Local Markdown needs no connector or account. Another destination needs the user's choice and tools with suitable access. A secret Gist is unlisted and is not access-controlled private storage.

Save the overview in the existing entry, and retain relevant detailed sources and discussions at their agreed locations. A concise page does not justify deleting originals. Under a full-history agreement, retain available original messages; a labeled summary can accompany them, not replace them. Preserve useful explanations, proposals, decisions and their basis, with speaker roles. On later updates, extend the source/history as well as changing the overview; label summaries and excerpts honestly.

When no storage choice exists, the three defaults in SKILL.md are presented once alongside the initial product synthesis. Continue useful collection while saving is pending. Changing the agreed location, scope, or detail level needs a new choice; a new session or product update does not.
